# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an **Automotive Sales Dashboard** developed using automotive sales data containing information related to:

- Car Brands
- Engine Types
- Pricing
- Dealer Ratings
- Safety Ratings
- Fuel Efficiency
- Revenue Performance
- Sales Trends

The dashboard converts raw automotive data into **actionable business insights**, helping stakeholders monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Automotive businesses require answers to important questions:

- Which brands generate the highest revenue?
- Which engine type performs best?
- How do sales trends change over time?
- Which factors drive profitability?
- Which segments deserve more investment?

Without proper visualization, identifying patterns becomes difficult.

This dashboard solves that problem by centralizing KPIs and trends.

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis & Key Insights

---

## 🥇 1. Brand Performance Analysis

### Observation:

Premium brands generated higher overall revenue.

### Insight:

Luxury vehicle brands tend to contribute significantly despite lower sales volumes.

### Business Recommendation:

Focus on:

- Premium positioning
- Customer retention
- Brand value enhancement

---

## ⚡ 2. Engine Type Performance

### Observation:

Electric and hybrid vehicles show increasing market contribution.

### Insight:

Consumer preference is shifting toward sustainable transportation.

### Business Recommendation:

Increase investments in:

- EV development
- Battery technology
- Green mobility solutions

---

## 📊 3. Sales Trend Analysis

### Observation:

Revenue trends indicate strong growth in recent years.

### Possible Reasons:

- Increased demand
- Better pricing strategies
- Expansion in premium segments
- Product diversification

### Business Impact:

Useful for:

- Demand forecasting
- Production planning
- Revenue prediction

---

## 💰 4. Revenue Drivers

Factors influencing revenue:

- Pricing Strategy
- Dealer Ratings
- Safety Ratings
- Brand Reputation
- Engine Type
- Fuel Efficiency
